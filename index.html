Built for the moment you're standing there, worried.

Hands full. Phone in one hand. Not sure if what you're looking at is nothing or an emergency. That's the moment Dave's Vet Station is for — a calm, knowledgeable voice that helps you decide what to do next.

Dave's Vet Station gives general guidance and is not a substitute for hands-on veterinary care. In a true emergency, contact your veterinarian or an emergency clinic right away. For pet poisonings, call ASPCA Animal Poison Control at 888-426-4435.

How it works

Three steps. No jargon.

1

Pick your vet

Cattle go to Dave. Dogs and cats go to Alice. Each one knows their own animals.

2

Say what you see

Plain language. Type it or talk it. Add a photo if it helps. No forms, no fuss.

3

Get a straight answer

What it might be, what to watch for, and whether it's time to call a real vet.
